GPA Calculator Lab

About GPA Calculator Lab

Updated November 23, 2026 · How we calculate

GPA Calculator Lab is a set of free calculators for U.S. high school students, college students and applicants. Each one shows its math: which grades counted, which didn't, and why.

Who we are

GPA Calculator Lab is built and maintained by FloPixels, a web design and SEO studio. We launched the site in November 2026. We're not a school, a counseling service or an admissions office. Our job is to turn published grading rules into calculators that get the arithmetic right and explain every step.

Who the calculators are for

We built the site for four kinds of visitors: high school students comparing their weighted and unweighted GPA before applications, college students planning a semester or a retake, applicants checking the UC or LSAC number before they submit, and parents or counselors who want to double-check a figure. If you're none of these and the site still helped, we'd like to hear what you used it for. Counselors and teachers can also add a calculator to their school website for free.

Why we built it

Most GPA calculators give you one number and stop. But the same grades can produce several GPAs: your school's weighted GPA, an unweighted GPA, a UC GPA capped at 8 honors points, and an LSAC GPA that counts every retake. Students run into the difference at the worst moment, on an application or a scholarship form. So we built a separate calculator for each set of rules, and every result lists the classes that counted and the ones that didn't.

How we check our math

  • Rules come from the organizations that set them: UC Admissions, LSAC, and the registrars and school districts we cite on each page. We link the source next to the rule and note the date we checked it.
  • Every calculator has automated tests built from worked examples, such as an A in a 3-credit class and a B in a 4-credit class giving 3.43, or 12 honors A's giving a capped UC GPA of 4.66. The site doesn't go live unless those tests pass.
  • Where schools differ, we say so and let you change the scale or the bonus points instead of guessing.

The formulas and sources for every calculator are on how our calculators work, along with a log of each change.

The calculators at a glance

  • High school GPA calculator: weighted and unweighted GPA, with your school's honors and AP bonus.
  • College GPA calculator: semester GPA by credit hours, with retakes handled the way your college handles them.
  • Cumulative GPA calculator: adds a new semester to the GPA and credits you already have.
  • UC GPA calculator: the University of California's capped and unweighted GPA from 10th and 11th grade.
  • LSAC GPA calculator: the GPA law schools see, with every attempt counted.
  • Grade, final grade and semester grade calculators: your grade in one class and the score you need on what's left.
  • Percentage to letter grade: where a percentage lands on the A–F scale.

What we cover, and what we don't

We cover U.S. grading: the 4.0 scale, high school weighting, and the UC and LSAC rules. We don't convert international grades such as IB scores or UK degree classifications. For those, the school you're applying to, or a credential evaluation service it names, gives the official number.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for an account, and we don't send the grades you type to our servers. The calculators run in your browser.
  • We don't publish ratings, reviews or user counts we can't back up.
  • We don't give admissions advice or promise what a GPA will get you. A result here is an estimate. Your school's transcript, or the official calculation by UC or LSAC, is the number that counts.

Get in touch

Found a mistake or a rule that's out of date? Contact us with the calculator name and the numbers you entered. We fix confirmed errors and note them in the changelog.
