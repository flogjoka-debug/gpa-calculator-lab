GPA Calculator Lab

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026. This policy explains what GPA Calculator Lab collects, what it doesn't, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The grades, classes and credits you type stay in your browser. We never send them to our servers or to analytics.
  • Google Analytics runs only if you click Accept on the cookie notice. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

Who we are

GPA Calculator Lab (gpacalculatorlab.com) is responsible for this website. It's built and maintained by FloPixels. For any privacy question, email info@gpacalculatorlab.com.

What stays on your device

The calculators work entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is saved unless you click "Save on this device", which stores your entries in this browser's local storage. "Clear saved data" removes them, and so does clearing your browser data.

When you follow a link such as "use this grade to plan your final", we keep that one percentage in your browser so the next page can fill it in. It's deleted after it's used or after 30 minutes.

We also store your cookie choice in local storage, so we don't ask again on every page.

We don't put grades in page addresses, so they don't end up in browser history, server logs or analytics. The one exception is a share link, and only when you click "Copy share link": it holds your classes after the # sign, a part of the address that browsers don't send to our server. When someone opens the link, the calculator fills in the classes and removes them from the address bar right away. Anyone you send a share link to can see the grades in it.

Google Analytics

If you accept, we load Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and calculators people use. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID) and collects information such as the pages you view, how you reached the site, your browser and device type, and your approximate location. We turn off Google signals and ad personalization, and we don't send Google any grades, names or other details you type.

Google processes this data on our behalf. You can read how in Google's privacy policy and opt out across sites with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

If you decline, Google Analytics doesn't load at all.

Server logs

Like every website, our hosting provider's servers record standard request logs, such as the address a request came from, the page requested, the time and the browser. These logs are used to keep the site running and secure. They don't contain the grades you type, because those never leave your browser.

Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to reply and to fix any error you report, and we don't add you to a mailing list. Please don't send full transcripts, student ID numbers or other personal records.

Children's privacy

The calculators don't ask for names, email addresses or any other personal information, so you can use them at any age without telling us who you are. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has emailed us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Withdraw or change your analytics consent at any time with "Cookie settings".
  • Delete anything saved in your browser with "Clear saved data" or your browser settings.
  • Ask us to access or delete an email you sent us, by writing to info@gpacalculatorlab.com.

We don't sell or share personal information for advertising. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you further rights, and you can use them by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

If we add a new tool that handles data differently, we'll update this page before the change goes live and change the effective date above.
