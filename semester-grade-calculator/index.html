How to use the semester grade calculator

  1. Pick your school's weighting, such as two quarters and an exam at 40/40/20, or choose Custom and enter the weights from your handbook.
  2. Enter each grading period's grade. Enter your exam score if you have it, or leave it blank.
  3. Press Calculate grade. With the exam filled in, you'll get your semester grade. Without it, you'll see the score you need on the exam for each letter.

How we calculated this

Each grading period and the exam has a weight. We multiply every grade by its weight, written as a decimal, and add the results. With 40/40/20, each quarter counts 0.40 and the exam 0.20.

How to calculate your semester grade

  1. Write down your grade for each quarter or grading period.
  2. Find how much each one counts, plus the semester exam. Your school's grading policy lists the split, such as 40/40/20.
  3. Multiply each grade by its weight written as a decimal.
  4. Add the results. That's your semester grade.

Example: 90, 80 and a 70 on the exam

0.40 × 90 = 36. 0.40 × 80 = 32. 0.20 × 70 = 14.

36 + 32 + 14 = 82, so the semester grade is 82%.

Looking for your semester GPA?

This page works out a grade for one class. If you want the GPA for all your classes this semester, use the College GPA Calculator or the High School GPA Calculator.

Common weightings

Many high schools use 40/40/20: two quarters worth 40% each and a final worth 20%. Some use 45/45/10, and some have no semester exam at all. Use Custom if your school splits the term into three grading periods or weights the exam differently.

Three grading periods and an exam

Schools on trimesters or six-week terms often split a semester into three periods. Pick Custom above and add a period for each one. Example: three periods worth 25% each and an exam worth 25%, with grades of 88, 92 and 85 and a 78 on the exam:

0.25 × 88 + 0.25 × 92 + 0.25 × 85 + 0.25 × 78 = 22 + 23 + 21.25 + 19.5 = 85.75%.

Semester grades without quarters

Some districts no longer average quarter grades. One Illinois district switched to a semester grade built from the full 18 weeks of work, weighted at 80%, plus the final exam at 20% (district comparison chart). To use this calculator that way, enter your semester average as one period worth 80% and the exam as 20%.

Passing two of three

Some schools add a rule on top of the average. Hillsborough County, for example, requires a passing grade on at least two of the three grades (two quarters and the exam) to earn credit. If you failed one quarter, check this rule even when your semester average is passing.

From semester grade to transcript

In many high schools, a yearlong course appears on the transcript as two semester grades, each worth half a credit. Your GPA then counts each semester on its own, so a B in the fall and an A in the spring add a 3.0 and a 4.0 instead of one averaged year grade. Other schools post a single grade for the whole year. Your transcript or student portal shows which way your school does it.

Once you have your semester letter grades, the high school GPA calculator turns them into a GPA, with the honors and AP bonus your school uses.

When the exam can't get you there

Leave the exam blank and the table also shows when a grade is out of reach. With a 40/40/20 split and quarter grades of 70 and 72, the A and B rows read "Not reachable": even a 100 on the exam brings the semester to 76.8. Knowing that early helps: your time may be better spent on a missing assignment, or on asking your teacher what can still change.

Your semester grade before the exam

Before the exam, your standing is the weighted average of your quarters alone. With quarters of 90 and 80 at 40% each, that's (0.4 × 90 + 0.4 × 80) ÷ 0.8 = 85. The exam then pulls the semester grade toward its own score, by as much as its weight.

How much the exam weight matters

Different schools give the semester exam different weights, and that changes how much one test can move your grade. With quarters of 90 and 80 and a 60 on the exam:

How much the exam weight matters
Exam weightQuarter weightsSemester grade
10%45% each82.5

The lower the exam's weight, the closer your semester grade stays to your quarter average of 85.

From semester grades to a year grade

Some schools post one grade for a full-year course instead of two semester grades. When both semesters count equally, the year grade is their average: an 88 and an 82 make an 85. Other schools weight the semesters or the final exam differently, so check your handbook before you rely on it.

Mistakes that throw off a semester grade

  • Weights that don't add up to 100%. Two quarters at 40% and an exam at 20% is fine; 45, 45 and 20 is not.
  • Entering a letter where the calculator needs a percentage. A B+ is a range, not a score, so use the number from your gradebook.
  • Using the wrong exam weight. Some schools give the semester exam less weight in one subject than another, or let some students skip it.
  • Expecting your grade to be rounded. Some schools round a semester average such as 89.5 up to 90 and others don't, so check your handbook before you count on it.

What to do before the semester exam

Leave the exam blank and calculate: the table shows the score you need for each letter. If the score you need for the next letter up is within reach, that's where your study time pays off. If it's out of reach, check that the letter you already have is safe, and put the rest of your time into the classes where the exam can still change the outcome.

Frequently asked questions

Is a semester grade the same as a final grade?

Often, yes. At many high schools the semester grade is the grade that goes on your transcript and into your GPA. The quarter grades are steps along the way.

How much is a semester exam usually worth?

Many high schools on a quarter system count it as 10% to 20% of the semester grade. Your school's grading policy has the exact number.

Can I use three grading periods?

Yes. Choose Custom, add a grading period, and enter each period's weight. The weights need to add up to 100%.

What if my school doesn't have a semester exam?

Pick "2 quarters, no exam (50/50)" or set the exam weight to 0. The semester grade is then the weighted average of your quarters.

How do you calculate a semester grade without the final?

Use your quarter grades alone: multiply each by its weight, add them and divide by the weights you used. Quarters of 90 and 80 at 40% each give (36 + 32) ÷ 0.8 = 85.

What is a semester average?

It's your grade for the whole semester: the weighted average of your grading periods and, if your school has one, the semester exam.

How do I calculate a year grade from two semesters?

If both semesters count equally, average them: an 88 and an 82 make an 85. If your school posts each semester separately, your transcript keeps two grades instead of one.
