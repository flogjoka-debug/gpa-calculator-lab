Free GPA Calculator for Your School Website

Updated November 23, 2026 · How we calculate

Counselors, advisors and teachers can put our GPA calculator on their own page for free. Copy the code below into your site, or link to the calculator that fits your students.

What students get

  • Semester and cumulative GPA, with an optional GPA so far from their transcript
  • A high school mode with Honors, AP, IB and dual enrollment bonus points you can set to your school's values
  • A target planner that shows the average they need next term
  • The math behind every result, so they can check it against their transcript

Add the calculator to your page

Paste this code where you want the calculator to appear. It works in most website builders that accept an HTML or embed block. Keep the credit link under the calculator: it tells students where the tool comes from and where to find the other calculators.

Preview

Prefer a link?

If your site doesn't allow embedded tools, a plain link works just as well. The pages students use most are the GPA calculator, the high school GPA calculator, the college GPA calculator, the final grade calculator and the UC GPA calculator.

Privacy for your students

The calculator runs in the student's browser. Grades aren't sent to us or to you, the embedded version doesn't load analytics, and there are no ads. The details are in our privacy policy.

Questions or a different scale?

If your school uses bonus points or a grade scale the calculator doesn't cover, tell us. We'd rather fix the tool than have students get the wrong number.

Frequently asked questions

Is the GPA calculator widget really free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no fee and no ads. Keep the credit link under the calculator so students know where the tool comes from.

Does the embedded calculator collect student data?

No. Grades are calculated in the student's browser and aren't sent to us or to your school, and the embedded version doesn't load analytics.

Will it match our school's weighting?

Students can set the Honors, AP and IB bonus points your school uses. If your school uses a scale or rule the calculator doesn't cover, tell us and we'll look at adding it.

Can we link to a calculator instead of embedding it?

Of course. You can link to any page on the site without asking, including the calculator that fits your students best.
