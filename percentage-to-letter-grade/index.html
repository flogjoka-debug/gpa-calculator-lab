How to use the percentage to letter grade calculator

  1. Enter your percentage, or switch to points and enter the points you earned and the points possible.
  2. Pick a scale: the common plus and minus scale, a straight 10-point scale, or Custom with the cutoffs from your syllabus.
  3. Convert. You'll see your letter grade, the range it covers and how many points you are from the next letter.

Does 89.99% count as an A?

On the common scale, no. An A− starts at 90, so 89.99% is a B+. We don't round before looking up the letter, because many teachers don't either. If your syllabus says grades are rounded, or that an A− starts at 89.5, switch to Custom and enter that cutoff. Both the result and the table will follow your scale.

How we converted your percentage

We compare your exact percentage with the cutoffs of the scale you picked. Each letter covers everything from its own cutoff up to, but not including, the next one. So on the common scale a B+ is 87 to under 90, and 87.00 is already a B+.

How to turn points into a percentage

Divide the points you earned by the points possible, then multiply by 100. 42 out of 50 is 42 ÷ 50 = 0.84, or 84%. Switch the calculator to points and it does this step for you.

Example: 42 out of 50

42 ÷ 50 = 0.84, which is 84%. On the common scale that's a B, because a B runs from 83 to under 87. A B+ starts at 87%, which on this test means 43.5 out of 50.

Common scale vs. 10-point scale

Many US schools split each letter into plus, plain and minus, with an A at 93 and above. Others use a straight 10-point scale where 90 to 100 is an A and there are no pluses or minuses. Your syllabus decides. The scale you choose here controls both the table and the result.

Does 89.5% round up to an A?

Only if your teacher or school rounds, and many don't. Some gradebooks round to the nearest whole number, some round only at the end of the term, and some never round. Your syllabus usually says which. This calculator uses your exact percentage, so 89.5% is a B+ on the common scale and a B on the 10-point scale. If your teacher rounds, enter the rounded number. When in doubt, ask your teacher before the last assignment, not after grades are posted.

From letter grade to GPA

Once you have the letter, the 4.0 scale gives its grade points: A 4.0, B 3.0, C 2.0, D 1.0, F 0, with plus and minus grades in between. The full chart is in the 4.0 GPA scale guide. To combine several letter grades into a GPA, use the college GPA calculator. How plus and minus grades work

On the common scale, each letter from D to B is split into three: the bottom three points are a minus, the middle four are the plain letter and the top three are a plus. So 80 to under 83 is a B−, 83 to under 87 is a B and 87 to under 90 is a B+. The A range is different: 90 to under 93 is an A−, and 93 and above is an A. A few schools give an A+ at 97 or above, so check your syllabus before you count on one.

A test letter isn't the same as a class letter. Your class grade comes from all your scores, weighted the way your syllabus says, and only that class letter goes into your GPA. The grade calculator combines your scores into one class grade.

Why your teacher's letter might differ

  • Your class uses different cutoffs, such as a B starting at 80 instead of 83.
  • Your teacher rounds, so an 89.5 becomes a 90.
  • Scores are curved or adjusted before the letter is given.
  • Your gradebook weights categories, so the overall percentage isn't a simple average of your tests.

Frequently asked questions

What letter grade is an 85?

On the common scale an 85 is a B, because a B runs from 83 to under 87. On a 10-point scale it's also a B.

What letter grade is a 79.5?

On the common scale a 79.5 is a C+, because a C+ runs from 77 to under 80. If your teacher rounds to the nearest whole number, it becomes an 80, which is a B−.

Is a 69 a D?

On the common scale a 69 is a D+, which runs from 67 to under 70. On a 10-point scale it's a D.

What percentage is a passing grade?

Many schools treat 60% as the lowest passing grade, but some courses and programs require 70% or more. Check your syllabus.

What percentage is a B?

On the common scale, a B is 83 to under 87, a B+ is 87 to under 90 and a B− is 80 to under 83. On a straight 10-point scale, any score from 80 to 89.99 is a B.

What percentage is an A?

On the common scale, an A is 93 and above and an A− is 90 to under 93. On a 10-point scale, 90 and above is an A.

What letter grade is an 87?

A B+ on the common scale and a B on a 10-point scale.
