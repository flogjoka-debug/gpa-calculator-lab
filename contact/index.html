GPA Calculator Lab

Contact GPA Calculator Lab

Spotted a wrong result or a rule that's out of date? Email info@gpacalculatorlab.com. Every message is read by the FloPixels team that builds and maintains the calculators.

What you can write to us about

  • A result that looks wrong. Tell us which calculator, the grades and credits you entered, and the number you expected.
  • A rule that has changed, such as a new UC or LSAC policy or a different grading scale at your school. A link to the official source helps us confirm it quickly.
  • Something that's hard to use on your phone or with a screen reader.
  • Questions about privacy, or a request to delete an email you sent us.
  • Press, partnership or classroom-use questions.

What to leave out

Please don't send full transcripts, student ID numbers, Social Security numbers or other personal records. We only need the numbers that went into the calculator.

Before you write

Many questions about why a result differs from your school's number are answered on how our calculators work, which lists the formula and source for every calculator. If you're comparing GPAs for an application, the GPA guides explain why the same grades can produce different numbers.

If your school's number is different

A different result isn't always an error. Your school may round each semester grade before averaging, use a 90/80/70/60 scale, or give a different bonus for honors classes. Check the scale and bonus settings in the calculator first. If the numbers still don't match, send us both results and we'll look at where they split.

What happens next

We check reported errors against the calculator's tests and the official source. When we fix something, we update the calculator and add the change to the changelog on the methodology page. You can read more about who builds the site.
